LAPL
Laser-Activated Platelet Lysate
The Science

Why low-level laser activation matters.

Low-level laser exposure triggers exocytosis from platelets, releasing a concentrated, cell-free cocktail of growth factors, ATP, and exosomes — with measurably higher BDNF than unactivated platelet lysate.

1

Acellular & Defined

No cells or debris — just a validated cytokine profile with verified sterility and endotoxin levels.

2

Stable & Shippable

3

Triple Mechanism

Reduces inflammation, provides analgesia, and drives tissue and nerve regeneration in a single formulation.
